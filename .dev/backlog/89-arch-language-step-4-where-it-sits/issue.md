# Architecture language step 4: say where an element sits

Step 4 of the architecture language's seven-step road, after step 3 (what an element is made of).

**The sentence this step buys:** `IGuard` sits in the `Abstractions` package.

**What it is.** The ladder of containment Tim asked after: assembly, package, namespace, entity, member. It existed in the design this language replaced and was not carried over, so nothing in it may be reasoned from — it has to be designed again and approved.

A change to the architecture language alone. No new word in MetaLang is known to be needed.
