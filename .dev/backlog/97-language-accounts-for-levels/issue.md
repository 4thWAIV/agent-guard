Tim flagged this while the remaining-language proposal was being written: the architecture language must be able to account for the levels a piece of work runs at. He said he would come back to it and said not to start it. This issue exists so it is tracked rather than carried in a document.

**What it is.** A value that varies along a named dimension. The workflow model already writes it — `l1: required, l2: required, l3: absent` — and so does the per-language table in the archived language design, `equivalents: {csharp: interface, python: Protocol or ABC, …}`. The corpus reading in `.dev/inprocess/82-guard-process-workflow/rebuild/GENERAL-FORM.md` found both to be the same statement shape and counted roughly 190 cells of it, making it the second largest body of content in the corpus after a thing written down with named parts.

**Where it stands.** MetaLang has `map`, added on 2026-10-25, which writes one of a thing for each word of a closed set. Whether that already answers this, or whether levels need something beyond it, has not been worked out.

**Not approved and not started.** Nothing here is a decision. It is not part of the five decisions currently open in the remaining-language proposal, and it is not on the seven-step road.
