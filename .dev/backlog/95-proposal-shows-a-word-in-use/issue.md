Found and fixed on 2026-10-26, after Tim said he could not decide from definitions alone and asked repeatedly for a usage example inside each item.

**What was wrong with the proposal language.** A `decision` carried the word, its parts, what it means, the exact lines, what you would read, what the checker refuses, the recommendation, the alternative, what happens if it is not taken and what it costs. It carried nowhere to show the word actually written down. The one device that draws a real thing — `reading` — was a top-level declaration, so every drawing was collected into the page's second section while every decision sat in the third. A reader was left holding one against the other himself, which is exactly the work a proposal exists to save him.

**The change.** `decision` gains `used`: a list of `inuse`, a third thing deriving from `shown`, drawn inside the decision under "The word in use", between what the word means and the lines that add it. It accepts `none`, so a decision adding no word says so out loud rather than being silently without one.

**What it needed underneath.** A word that does not exist yet cannot be drawn out of a document that uses it, because no such document checks. The way through is MetaLang's own derivation: a scaffold declares an entity deriving from the real one and carrying the proposed property, and a second document declares real parts of this repository as values of it. Both check, so the drawing is of a document that really exists rather than of lines nothing reads. `handoff/remaining/arch_lines.yaml` holds the vocabularies and the derived entities; `handoff/in_use.yaml` holds the worked examples, kept apart so a drawn card is not preceded by every example those vocabularies have not yet been given.

**One thing to know when writing the next one.** A document importing a scope does not reach what that scope itself imports. `handoff/in_use.yaml` has to bring in `[arch]` directly even though the scaffold it imports already does, or the derivation chain stops and every value reads as deriving down to no primitive. That is scoping working as written rather than a defect, but it is not obvious from the error.
