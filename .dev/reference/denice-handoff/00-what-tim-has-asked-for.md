# What Tim has asked for, in his own terms

This is the most recent material in the package and carries the most weight. Everything here was stated by Tim on 2026-11-06, relayed through his jarvis session. It is recorded as his stated intent, not as an agreed design, except where it says a decision was taken.

## The eight controls

Tim is designing Denice's user-facing work model around eight concepts, which he named in this order:

1. inputs
2. outputs
3. dependencies
4. events
5. attention
6. workflows
7. steps
8. state machines

Throughout this package, "the planned controls" means those eight. They are Tim's words; no definition of any of them has been agreed, and this repository holds no design for Denice's user-facing model. `02-controls-against-the-language.md` reports what the language in this repository can and cannot say about each of the eight today.

## The three instructions he gave about them

**He asked for the actual state of the language before connecting these ideas to it, and told me not to assume a design he had not given.** The answer he was given: MetaLang and three languages written in it are built and checked; the evolutionary language, EvoLang, has one recorded description from 2026-10-08 and nothing approved or written.

**He then asked for the intended destination of EvoLang** — what it is meant to express, and how its nouns, verbs, grammar, declarations and evaluation would work — so he can plan Denice's user-facing work model around it, with practical examples for each of the eight controls and a clear line between established intent and open questions.

**He asked for this package to be prepared and handed to the Denice session so it can start work**, organised by relevance and in date order, with the most recent decisions weighted highest, and with every reference to this repository included.

## The standing rules that govern any answer about the language

Both are Tim's, and both are still in force.

- Every word of the language is approved by him one at a time. Nothing enters the vocabulary without that approval.
- No language feature is raised until a problem cannot be solved without it. A gap in the language is reported as a gap; it is not filled with a proposal.

A consequence for Denice: where this package says the language has no word for one of the eight controls, that is a statement of fact and not an invitation to design one. A word for any of them would need Tim's approval first, and he has not been asked for one.

## The purpose the language is being built toward, settled 2026-11-06

EvoLang exists to describe an obligation about work: what must be true, who must make it so, under what condition, and how anyone would know it happened. Tim approved that on 2026-11-06 and said it is the entire purpose.

That closes a fork that had stood for three weeks. Two statements of purpose were on the record — the ruling of 10 October, that the language exists to provide the analyzer rules, the tests and the implementation plan, and the cooperative purpose of 19 October, that EvoLang is the language in which one session asks another to do work on its behalf. They are one subject at two scales, and neither is withdrawn.

Because this is the entire purpose, it is also a bound. EvoLang is not a general language for saying true things. Every sentence it writes is a sentence about work: an obligation, its condition, the party answerable for it, and the evidence that would settle it. A proposed word earns its place against that test, which is narrower than expressiveness.

The eight controls sit inside that purpose rather than on a boundary between two of them.
